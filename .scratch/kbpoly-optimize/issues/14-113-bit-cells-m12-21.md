# 14: 113-bit table cells for m 12 to 21

Status: needs-triage

Part of: PRD addendum (2026-11-06). Depends on: issue 12.

## Problem

The 113-bit cells `m = 12 .. 21` (39 cells) are not generated. The committed
header caps `KB_POLY_OPT_MMAX` at 11 in the 113-bit block, so 113-bit plans
with `m > 11` get no exact hit and solve from scratch in software arithmetic
(on aarch64 the trafo test did not finish its sigma 1.1, m 16 plan in
24 min).

Measured cost of the generator:

- A `-j 10` run exhausted the 7.8 GB host; `m = 11` workers exceed 1.3 GB.
- One `m = 21` cell (sigma 1.1, h 9) did not finish round 0 in 15 min; the
  estimate is hours per cell.
- The 104-cell `--mmax 11 -j 3` run took 10527 s wall.

In that run one cell, sigma 3.0, m 11, h 8 (the 80-bit degree), failed the
exchange gap (e 1.4695e-24, lo 7.0996e-25): the exchange stopped on a window
with `psi_hat <= 0` off the design. The generator repair (feed those `xi`
into the design, restart from the best window) is landing on this branch,
and the header is regenerated after it.

## Options

A larger host, a faster IPM (structure of the normal matrix per frequency),
or fewer rows for `m > 9` where the denser design of the generator triples
them.

## Acceptance

All 127 cells of the 113-bit range in the header with `e <= 1.005 lo`, and
`kbpoly_table` passing in the 113-bit tree up to m 21.
